Where cost actually hides

Four places we look before we ever touch a renewal number.

Most renewals are a single lever: the premium. We treat premium as the output of several levers pulled together.

Funding & risk structure

The single biggest lever most fully-insured employers never test.

  • Level-funded and self-funded modeling
  • Captive participation for mid-size groups
  • Stop-loss placement & renewal negotiation

Pharmacy

Often the fastest-growing line item on the claims report — and the least transparent.

  • PBM carve-out evaluation
  • Rebate transparency & pass-through review
  • Specialty and GLP-1 utilization strategy

Network & steerage

Where care is delivered matters as much as what's covered.

  • Reference-based pricing
  • Direct contracting with local systems
  • Centers of excellence & bundled steerage

Beyond benefits

Health benefits are usually the second-largest expense. They're rarely the only one worth a second look.

  • Telecom contract audits
  • Energy procurement review
  • Vendor & recurring-spend audits
Partner network

We're not experts in telecom. We know who is.

A meaningful part of cost containment sits outside insurance entirely. Rather than stretch our own team thin, we bring in specialist partners for contract and spend audits — telecom, energy, and vendor agreements are common starting points — and manage the relationship so it's one more thing off an employer's plate, not one more vendor to coordinate.

  • Specialist partners, vetted and coordinated by Verus
  • No cost to explore — most audits are contingency-based
  • One point of contact, regardless of how many specialists are involved
