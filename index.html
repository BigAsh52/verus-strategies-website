The default vs. the alternative

Most agencies present renewals. We rebuild them.

Fully-insured plans get shopped once a year and priced up every year after. That's not a strategy — it's a mailing schedule. Somewhere between "cut benefits" and "eat the increase," there's a third option most employers never see.

Industry default

Renew, absorb, repeat

  • ×Same fully-insured carrier shopped once a year
  • ×Plan design left untouched to avoid disruption
  • ×Increase passed to the employer, or benefits quietly trimmed
  • ×Funding structure, stop-loss, and Rx never revisited
  • ×Cost containment treated as a once-a-decade project
The Verus approach
